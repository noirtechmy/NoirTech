Service 03

Reporting dashboards & automation

What a dashboard engagement delivers

This is our newest service line, so there is no public client project to show yet. Rather than pad this page, here is exactly what the engagement involves and what you receive at the end of it.

Consolidate

We map where your numbers actually live — exports, shared drives, systems that do not talk to each other — and use Power Query to pull them into one clean source.

Build

A Power BI dashboard structured around the decisions you make each month, not around every metric that happens to be available.

Hand over

One-click refresh, so the report is never rebuilt by hand again. Plus a walkthrough so your team can run and extend it without calling us.
